Privacy Policy

How the ClearFormula mobile app handles information and user-submitted content.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

For privacy questions, contact clearformula.app@gmail.com.

1. Scope and contact

This Privacy Policy describes how ClearFormula processes information and user-submitted content in the ClearFormula mobile application. The developer name is ClearFormula. For privacy questions, data requests and deletion requests, please contact clearformula.app@gmail.com.

2. Purpose of the app

ClearFormula helps users look up cosmetic products, barcodes and INCI ingredient lists and provides informational assessments based on selected profiles. These assessments are not medical diagnoses or treatment advice.

3. Categories of information

  • Optional missing-product submissions: Product or ingredient-label photographs, brand and product name, barcode and INCI text provided by a user.
  • Product searches: Product, barcode and ingredient queries may be processed by our servers to return relevant information. We do not intend to create a persistent personal search-history profile.
  • On-device selections: Profile choices, favorites, used products and personal preferences are used to personalize the app experience. Information stored locally on the device is not affected by the server cleanup of missing-product submissions.
  • Technical access information: Infrastructure providers may process connection information, including IP addresses and error information, to deliver and secure the service.
  • Support correspondence: If you email us, your email address and message may be processed so we can respond to your request.

4. Photos and camera

Camera and photo access is used only for actions initiated by the user, such as barcode scanning, product lookup or optional missing-product submissions. Product lookup is available without submitting photos. Please avoid including faces, documents or other people’s private information in submitted photos.

5. Why we process information

We process information to answer product and ingredient queries, review missing-product submissions, verify formulas, improve catalog accuracy, maintain technical security and respond to support requests. We do not intend to use submitted product photos for targeted advertising or marketing.

6. Service providers and international processing

ClearFormula uses Supabase to host its product database and process missing-product submissions. Supabase provides hosting and data-processing services on our behalf. Our project is hosted in a Japan server region, so relevant information may be processed outside Türkiye. We do not sell user-submitted photos to third parties for their independent advertising purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

Missing-product submission records and their related photos that are more than 10 days old are selected for removal by a daily automated cleanup task. Because the task runs daily, deletion may take place within the following 24 hours. Verified non-personal product and INCI information incorporated into the public catalog may be kept as catalog data. Backups and security records may be subject to the infrastructure provider’s technical retention schedules. On-device favorites and profiles are not affected by this server cleanup.

8. Security

Connections with Supabase use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. Storage for user-submitted missing-product photos is configured as private. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights and requests

To request access, correction or deletion of data we process about you, or to ask privacy questions, email clearformula.app@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable information to identify the relevant record and validate the scope of your request. Your rights under applicable data-protection law remain unaffected.

10. Age and updates

ClearFormula is intended for people aged 16 and over. Child and baby profiles are intended to help users assess products for children, not to encourage children to use the app themselves. We may revise this policy to reflect changes to the app and will update the revision date when significant changes are made.
